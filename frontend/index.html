National Statistical Competency Framework

1. Statistical Methods & Sampling

National accounts compilation (SNA 2008), survey design (PLFS, ASUSE), macroeconomic indicators, and index numbers (CPI, IIP).

2. Technical & Analytical Tools

CAPI tablet data collection, Python/Pandas data wrangling, R statistical computing, SQL microdata warehousing, and GIS analytics.

3. Digital Governance & Policy

Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, respondent confidentiality, Government Cloud Security, and metadata standards.

4. Leadership & Administration

Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH), e-Office records lifecycle, public procurement (GeM), and evidence-based decision making.
